# About

The **ML Morning Digest** is a daily news briefing written from a Marxist-Leninist perspective. Each morning it picks the day's most important world stories, with a standing focus on Venezuela, the DPRK and Palestine, and for each one it looks at:

- 🔍 **Framing**: how outlets across the spectrum describe the same events, and the word choices that reveal their angle.
- 🧭 **Sourcing flags**: where key claims actually come from, such as government-funded media, state officials or funded think tanks, with the link stated.
- ⛏️ **Dig deeper**: documents, reports and data worth following up.
- ⚖️ **Counterarguments**: the strongest objections, a rebuttal, and an honest verdict on which side the evidence favours right now.
- 🕳️ **Blind spots**: what the analysis or its sources may be missing.
- 📕 **ML analysis**: the material interests, class forces and history behind the story.

The digest is first posted to Discord and then archived here, so every edition has a permanent link and can be searched. Subscribe with the [RSS feed]({{ '/feed.xml' | relative_url }}).
